Export a .shdo v2 file from Studio, then share a stripped-down reusable copy. Nothing is published until you confirm.

You must own or have permission to license all content in the template. A licence cannot recall copies already downloaded.
Public sharing is permanent unless you unshare it. Your displayed creator name, title, description, reuse licence and sanitised show template will be visible publicly. We remove hardware bindings, asset paths and other known private fields. Review your own scene and clip names before publishing.

Templates, not live commands
