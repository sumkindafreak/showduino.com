HAUNTSYNC

Community guidelines.

HauntSync is for Showduino users, haunt designers, immersive creators, makers and electronics enthusiasts. These rules keep it useful and safe.

Taking part

  • Anyone can read the community. To post, comment, follow or share show files, sign in with your Showduino ID and publish a creator profile. Your posts show the creator name and username you chose there. Any signed-in member who is not suspended can report.
  • Be constructive and kind. Critique builds, not people.
  • Share your own work, or work you have permission to share. Credit others.
  • No illegal content, spam, advertising without context, harassment, hateful or sexual content, threats, or impersonation of other people or of Showduino.
  • Do not post other people's personal information, or private photos of anyone.
  • Names such as "admin", "founder", "moderator", "official", "Showduino" and "HauntSync" are reserved and cannot be used by members, including look-alike spellings.

Safety

  • Showduino's emergency features are entertainment show-control functions. They are not certified life-safety equipment and do not replace fire alarms, emergency lighting, exits or a qualified safety assessment.
  • Do not post instructions that put people at risk. Mains-voltage wiring belongs with a qualified electrician; say so when you share such projects.
  • Shared .shdo show files are templates. They never contain your hardware addresses, and loading one does not operate any hardware until you deploy it yourself.

Reporting

Use Report on any post, comment, shared show or creator profile. Reports go only to the Showduino founder. The person you report is not told who reported them. You can report each item once.

Anyone, with or without an account, can report content that may be illegal by emailing showduino38@gmail.com. Content that appears to be illegal is removed as soon as we become aware of it.

How moderation works

  • Hidden or removed: the item disappears for everyone except its author and the founder. The author sees the reason. Hidden items can be restored.
  • Restricted: the member can read and edit their profile, but cannot post, comment or share for a while. They can still report problems.
  • Suspended: the member's profile, posts and shows are hidden and they cannot take part.
  • Every moderation decision is recorded in an audit log.
  • Restrictions and suspensions are decided by the founder, not by an automated tool. Spam limits only pause posting for a short time.
  • To appeal a decision, email showduino38@gmail.com with "Appeal" in the subject, or use the Support page. The founder reviews every appeal and replies with the outcome. See complaints and appeals.

Limits

To keep spam out there are limits per account: 5 posts per 10 minutes and 20 per day, 20 comments per 10 minutes and 150 per day, 5 shared shows per day, 10 reports per hour and 100 follows per hour. Images must be JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 3 MB for post images and 2 MB for profile photos.

Each account can also keep up to 100 cloud shows (50 new a day), 200 saved devices (100 new a day), 5 stored profile photos (10 uploads a day) and 200 post images (20 a day). Saving changes to a show you already have never counts against the limit. If you need more, contact support.

Privacy

Your private Studio projects and saved hardware are never visible in the community. Only the profile details you publish, your posts, images, comments and shared shows are public. Who you follow is shown only while both creator pages are published. Unpublishing your creator profile hides your profile and your posts. Copies others have already downloaded may remain. See the privacy notice.
