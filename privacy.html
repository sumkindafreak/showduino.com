This notice covers the website, Showduino Studio in your browser, Showduino ID accounts and the HauntSync community. Showduino hardware (the P4 Show Engine, Communications S3, Director and nodes) runs your show on your own network and does not send show or personal data to Showduino.

What is public

  • Your account and profile are private until you press Publish on your profile page. Publishing creates a public copy of your username, display name, biography, creative focus, optional website and optional photo. Your email address, private projects, saved hardware and password are never published.
  • Posts, comments and shared show files are public and show your creator name and username. Who you follow is visible to others only while both your creator page and theirs are published.
  • Profile photos and post images are served from public image addresses. Anyone with an image's address can view it. Do not upload private or sensitive images.
  • When you share a show file, HauntSync publishes a copy with known hardware identifiers, asset paths and other private fields removed. Titles, cue labels and parameter values can still reveal details, so check the file first.
  • Removing or unpublishing content stops it being shown on HauntSync. Copies other people have already downloaded, saved or cached may remain.
  • Only you and the Showduino founder can see your own reports, member status and the reasons for any moderation decision about your content. The person you report is not told who reported them.

Who handles data for us

We use these providers to run the service. They act on our instructions under their data processing terms.

  • Supabase: database, accounts and image storage. Our database is hosted in London (AWS eu-west-2).
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, content delivery, security and, when switched on, Turnstile bot protection.
  • Email delivery: confirmation and password-reset emails are sent through To confirm: the email delivery provider used by Supabase Auth.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the open-source Supabase client library to your browser, so it receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Google: Google sign-in is not currently offered. If it is added, this notice will be updated before it goes live.

Some of these providers, or their support teams and sub-processors, may handle data outside the UK. To confirm: the transfer safeguard relied on for each provider, for example UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Links to other sites, such as GitHub or hardware retailers, are covered by those sites' own privacy notices.

Storage on your device

Studio and HauntSync keep some information in your browser so they work: your sign-in session, local projects and autosaves, the active project, registered devices, sounds you imported, Studio settings and the address of your Showduino controller. This is strictly necessary for features you use, so we do not ask for cookie consent. It stays on your device unless you save a project to your account. Clearing site data removes it; export important shows as .shdo backups first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data (access);
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to uses based on legitimate interests;
  • give you data you provided in a reusable format (portability).

You can already edit your profile, unpublish your creator page, remove your photo, delete your own posts and comments, unshare show files, and export projects as .shdo files. For anything else, email showduino38@gmail.com from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within one month, or tell you within that month if a complex request needs up to two more.

Deleting your account

You can request account deletion from Account or Profile. An explicit confirmation starts a 30-day grace period, during which your account stays fully usable. Signing in does not cancel or extend deletion. To keep the account, press Cancel deletion before the deadline. After the grace period, automated cleanup removes the live account, profile, creator page, cloud projects, saved hardware, posts, comments, follows, shared shows and stored images. Cleanup normally runs within five minutes of the deadline and retries service failures. Local exports and copies downloaded by others are unaffected. Reports and moderation/security records may remain for the retention periods above. To confirm: how long deleted data remains in provider backups. You may still contact showduino38@gmail.com about your data rights.

Age

You must be 14 or older to create a Showduino ID. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone below that age.

Security

Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider. Database rules limit each account to its own private data, and moderation tools are available only to the single Showduino founder account, identified by a fixed account ID rather than a name or email. No system is perfectly secure: use a unique password, and tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, email showduino38@gmail.com with "Data protection complaint" in the subject. We will acknowledge it within 30 days and tell you the outcome without undue delay. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Payments

Payments are switched off. The Showduino ID is free. Starter, Pro and Studio memberships are proposed but not on sale. If paid plans launch, this notice will be updated first.
