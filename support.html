Common questions

Does Showduino work offline?

Yes. Studio project saving, importing, exporting and local HauntSync project management are designed to work without cloud access.

Are payments active?

No. Stripe and subscription services remain disabled while the website is being completed and tested.

Where are projects stored?

Until cloud services are enabled, projects are stored in your browser and can be exported as `.shdo` backups.
