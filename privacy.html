Privacy notice

How Showduino handles information while the website is under active development.

Current website operation

Showduino Studio and HauntSync currently store projects, device inventories and preferences locally in your browser. This information is not uploaded while Firebase and cloud synchronization remain disabled.

Account services

Account registration and Firebase-backed cloud services are prepared but disabled. This notice will be updated before those services are activated to explain the exact information collected, why it is required and how long it is retained.

Payments

Payment and subscription features are disabled. No card details are collected or processed by the current website.

Browser storage

The website uses browser local storage to retain projects, autosaves, device records and interface settings. You can remove this information through your browser settings. Export important projects as `.shdo` files before clearing site data.

Support messages

The support form opens your own email application. The website does not upload or store the message before you choose to send it.

Third-party links

The website may link to GitHub or other external services. Their own privacy policies apply when you leave Showduino.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to showduino38@gmail.com.
