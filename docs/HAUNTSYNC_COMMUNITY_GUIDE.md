# HauntSync community: user guide (W004)

The public, shorter version of the rules is on the site at `guidelines.html`. This guide is for
support and documentation writers.

## Getting started
1. **Create an account.** Use the free Showduino account at **Account**, with email and password or Google sign-in where configured.
2. **Build your profile.** Go to **HauntSync → My Profile** and add a display name, username, bio, specialism, website and photo. The photo can be uploaded, previewed, replaced or removed; without one, your initials are shown.
3. **Publish it.** Press **Publish my creator page**. Until you do, nothing about you is public. A published profile is what lets you post, comment and share shows.

## Using the community
| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Community** (`feed.html`) | Latest, Following and Featured posts. Filter by category or type (Discussion, Build log, Showcase, Question), or search. **Write a post** to share text and an optional image (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 3 MB). |
| **Post pages** | Read and reply. Replies are one level deep. Edit or delete your own posts and comments; edited ones are marked. |
| **Creators** | The opt-in directory. A creator page shows the profile, Founder badge where it applies, follower, post and show counts, member-since month, recent posts and shared shows. Use **Follow** to see their posts under Following. |
| **Projects** (`community.html`) | Shared `.shdo` show templates. Previews and downloads never include hardware addresses, and nothing runs on hardware until you deploy it yourself in Studio. Each show is credited to its author. |
| **Report** | On posts, comments, shows and profiles. It goes to the founder only. You can report each item once. |

## Privacy
- **Private Studio projects** are never public.
- **Public:** your published profile, posts, comments, follows (only between published creators) and shared shows.
- **Unpublishing** your profile hides your profile, posts, comments, follows and shows from everyone else. Nothing is deleted.
- **Email** addresses are never shown, including on the founder's dashboard.

## Account states
| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | Normal |
| Restricted | Can read, report and edit the profile; cannot post, comment or share. A notice shows the reason. |
| Suspended | Profile and content hidden; cannot take part. A notice shows the reason. |

To ask for a review, use **Support**.

## Not implemented yet
- **Social links.** Only one website link is supported, not separate social links.
- **Images on comments.** Comments can't carry images.
- **Notifications.** There are none, by email or in the app.
- **Self-service account deletion.** Users have to ask through **Support**; release checklist item W-011 is still open.
- **`.shdo` sharing in a post.** Sharing shows stays in **Projects**. Posts can store a link to one of your own shared shows (`shared_show_id`), but the composer doesn't offer it yet.
